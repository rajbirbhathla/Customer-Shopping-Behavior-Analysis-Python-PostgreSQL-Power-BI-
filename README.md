# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis  
**Python | PostgreSQL | Power BI**

## Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to understand what drives revenue and purchasing patterns.

The goal was not just to process data, but to extract insights that could help a business improve customer retention, pricing strategy, and overall performance.

---

## Dataset
- ~3,900 customer transaction records  
- Includes:
  - Customer demographics  
  - Purchase history  
  - Product categories  
  - Payment methods  
  - Subscription status  

---

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python (pandas, numpy)** → data cleaning and preparation  
- **PostgreSQL** → querying and analysis  
- **Power BI** → dashboard and visualization  

---

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning (Python)
- Handled missing values  
- Standardized column formats  
- Created structured dataset for analysis  

### 2. Data Analysis (SQL)
Used SQL to answer key business questions:
- Which customers generate the most revenue?  
- How does subscription status impact spending?  
- What product categories perform best?  
- How do discounts affect purchasing behavior?  

### 3. Data Visualization (Power BI)
- Built an interactive dashboard  
- Visualized revenue trends, customer segments, and purchasing patterns  

---

## Key Insights

- **Revenue concentration**  
  A small group of repeat customers contributes a large share of total revenue.

- **Subscription impact**  
  Customers with subscriptions tend to spend more than non-subscribers.

- **Discount effectiveness**  
  Discounts do not consistently increase high-value purchases, suggesting they should be used selectively.

- **Customer behavior patterns**  
  Purchase behavior varies across segments, indicating opportunities for targeted strategies.

---

## Business Recommendations

- **Focus on retention**  
  Invest in keeping high-value customers rather than only acquiring new ones.

- **Promote subscriptions**  
  Encourage subscription models to increase average customer value.

- **Use targeted discounts**  
  Avoid blanket discounting—focus incentives on specific customer segments.

- **Segment customers**  
  Tailor marketing and product strategies based on behavior and spending patterns.
